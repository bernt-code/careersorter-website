How Shortlistme Works

From Messy CV to
Matched Candidate

Five steps. Multiple layers of intelligence. One outcome: your verified students connected directly with recruiters who need them.

1

Design School Template

We work with your careers team to create CV templates customised to your institutional branding. Your logo, your colours, your standards — applied consistently across every student.

Secret Sauce

ATS-proof formatting. No tables, no text boxes, no images that break parsers. Tested against every major Applicant Tracking System. Your students' CVs won't get rejected because of formatting.

2

Send to Students

Students receive access to Shortlistme. They upload their existing CV — any format, any mess, any language. Word docs, PDFs, even LinkedIn exports.

Self-service

Students work independently. Great for large cohorts.

CV Clinic Sessions

Live editing with coaches. Up to 500 students at once.

3

Convert & Structure

Powered by Allsorter

AI extracts all content from the uploaded CV. Guided prompts help students fill gaps. The system organises everything into your school-branded template automatically.

Secret Sauce

AI content tuning. The system doesn't just format — it improves. Skills extraction finds competencies students forgot to mention. Content suggestions help them articulate experience better. 36 languages supported.

4

Upload to Talent Pool

Your careers team reviews CVs, gives feedback, approves. Approved CVs enter your institution's searchable talent pool.

Secret Sauce

AI competency extraction. Each CV is analysed by AI that understands meaning. "10 years competitive sailing" becomes resilience, leadership, risk assessment. Every CV becomes a semantic embedding — searchable by what the person can do, not just keywords they used.

5

Connect to Recruiter

Recruiters access your talent pool. They describe what they need in natural language — "analytical thinker with cross-cultural experience" — and Shortlistme finds matches by meaning.

Secret Sauce

Semantic search + direct contact. No keyword matching that misses "strategy" because the CV says "strategic". No application forms. No ATS delays. Recruiter finds candidate, reaches out directly. You track what happens.
